The Problem

Finding a Room Shouldn't Be Difficult

Students and faculty currently rely on physical checks and manual records to find a free classroom or lab.

Wasted Time

Students and faculty spend unnecessary time walking through corridors to check whether a room is empty or occupied.

Double Booking

Without a centralized record, two different groups can book the same room at the same time, causing scheduling conflicts.

Scattered Information

Room schedules and availability details are spread across multiple places, making it difficult to get a clear picture.

The Solution

One Place to Check Room Availability

Search by Date & Time

Choose when you need a room and instantly see what is available across all buildings.

Centralized Room Data

All room information — schedules, capacity, and facilities — is available in one interface.

Conflict-Free Booking

The system prevents double booking by recording all reservations in a shared database.

Simple Process

How ROOMCHECK Works

01

Select Date & Time

Choose the date and time window you need the room for your activity.

02

Choose Room Type

Filter by room type — classroom or laboratory — and set your required capacity.

03

Check Availability

The system shows which rooms are available and which are currently occupied.

04

Choose Your Room

Select a suitable room, view its full schedule, and reserve it for your session.

Features

Everything You Need to Find the Right Room

Date & Time Search

Find rooms based on your required schedule. Set a start and end time to see what fits your needs.

Classroom & Lab Filter

Quickly narrow results by room type — whether you need a regular classroom or a computer laboratory.

Availability Status

Clearly identify which rooms are available and which are currently occupied at your chosen time.

Room Details

View each room's capacity, available facilities, location, and floor — all in one place.

Full-Day Schedule

Understand how a room is being used throughout the day before deciding to book it.

Alternative Suggestions

If your preferred room is occupied, the system suggests other suitable available rooms nearby.
